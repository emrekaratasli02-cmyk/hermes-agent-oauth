Privacy policy

This application is used by one person: its owner. It accesses Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, Contacts, YouTube) only for that owner's own account, on the owner's own computer.

No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, sold, or shared with anyone. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
